# Private Limited Company Incorporation: India vs. Estonia vs. Delaware
## Comparative Research Report for Founders — November 2026

---

## Executive Summary

A founder choosing between India, Estonia, and Delaware faces markedly different regulatory environments. **Estonia** is the fastest and simplest path to a fully digital private company (OÜ): one state fee of €265, approval in one working day, and no ongoing annual report filing fee — though a contact-person service (~€200–400/yr) is effectively mandatory for non-residents [11][13]. **Delaware LLC** is the fastest US option: one filing fee of $110, no annual report, and a flat $400/yr tax, though a registered agent (private cost, government fees set) is legally required [34][5][35]. **India** is the most complex: 5–6 sequential steps, fees that vary by share capital and state (stamp duty), and the richest ongoing compliance calendar — but it is the only option for a company that must actually operate on the ground in India [26][40].

## Section 1: India — Private Limited Company (Pvt Ltd)

### Governing Law & Authority
Companies Act, 2013; Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA); Registrar of Companies (ROC). Primary portal: [mca.gov.in](https://www.mca.gov.in).

> **Important fee caveat:** MCA waived incorporation government fees (ROC registration fee) for companies with authorised capital up to ₹15 lakh via the SPICe+ zero-fee scheme introduced 26 January 2019. The MCA "Enquire Fees" page notes all displayed figures are "indicative only" [9]. The binding fee is computed live at the time of SPICe+ filing. Stamp duty on MOA/AOA is a **State** levy, not a central fee, and varies sharply by state [40][45].

> **Stamp duty note:** No single central government stamp duty schedule exists for MOA/AOA. The MCA Rules explicitly state stamp duty is payable "in the respective State" [40]. ## Section 3: Delaware, United States — LLC or Corporation

### Governing Law & Authority
Delaware General Corporation Law (Title 8) for corporations; Delaware Limited Liability Company Act (Title 6, Chapter 18) for LLCs. Division of Corporations, Delaware Secretary of State. Primary portal: [corp.delaware.gov](https://corp.delaware.gov).

> **Critical 2026 fee update:** Delaware House Bill 400, signed 21 May 2026, increased numerous fees and taxes. Most filing/service fee increases took effect **1 August 2026**; annual entity tax increases are retroactive to **1 January 2026** [15][16][17][23][24]. The official fee schedule revised August 1, 2026 is at [corpfiles.delaware.gov/Fee_Schedule/AugustFee2026.pdf](https://corpfiles.delaware.gov/Fee_Schedule/AugustFee2026.pdf) [27]. The table below uses the post-August 2026 schedule.

> **Entity choice note:** This section covers both the **Delaware Corporation** (C-Corp, the standard VC-backed structure) and the **Delaware LLC** (simpler for small businesses). The founder should choose based on their capital-raising and tax needs.

> **LLC annual tax clarification (2026):** HB 400 raised the annual tax from $300 to $400 effective 1 January 2026. Because the tax is paid in arrears, the **first $400 payment is due June 1, 2027** (covering tax year 2026). The payment due June 1, 2026 (covering tax year 2025) remained $300 [15][17][23][24]. The official Division of Corporations LLC/LP/GP franchise tax instructions page and the official LLC Formation PDF both state **$400** as the current annual tax [5][34].

> **Corporation franchise tax note:** HB 400 did **not** increase the standard annual franchise tax rates for domestic corporations. Corporations may still see higher costs from increases to filing fees, annual report fees, and expedited service charges [15].

## Recommendation: Easiest Place to Start

### **Recommended: Estonia (OÜ) for a non-India-based founder; Delaware LLC for a US-operations founder**

**For a founder with no specific India nexus:** **Estonia** is the objectively simplest and fastest path:

- **One state fee** (€265), **one government step**, **one working day** to approval [11][12][20]
- No minimum capital beyond €0.01 [11]
- 100% digital — 15-minute application if documents are in order [20]
- No annual report filing fee [14]
- 0% corporate tax on retained/reinvested profits [20]
- No residency requirement for directors or shareholders

**For a founder targeting the US market or seeking VC funding:** **Delaware LLC** (or C-Corp for VC-backed startups) is standard. The LLC has the simplest ongoing burden ($400 flat annual tax, no annual report) [5][34].

**India is not "easiest" but is non-negotiable** if the company must operate physically in India — a foreign-registered company cannot substitute for an Indian entity when Indian regulatory compliance, GST registration, employment contracts, or Indian banking are needed.

## Trade-offs of Estonia (Easiest Choice)

| Factor | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Speed & simplicity ✅** | 1 working day to approval; 1 fee; fully digital [12][20] |
| **Cost ✅** | €265 at formation; no annual report fee [13][14] |
| **Tax efficiency ✅** | 0% on reinvested profits; 20% only on distributions from 2026 [13][20] |
| **e-Residency prerequisite ⚠️** | Non-EU founders must pay €150 for e-Residency and wait for card delivery before incorporation [13] |
| **Contact person cost ⚠️** | Effectively mandatory for non-residents: ~€200–400/yr private cost [13] |
| **Accounting cost ⚠️** | Annual report mandatory even for inactive companies; accountant from ~€50/month [13][14] |
| **Banking ⚠️** | Opening a business bank account from outside Estonia can be difficult; some founders use fintech alternatives (Wise, Revolut Business) — no official government source on this, UNCLEAR |
| **No physical presence benefit** | Estonian OÜ does not substitute for local registration in India or most US states if you operate there |
| **Limited VC ecosystem** | Estonian companies are less familiar to US VCs than Delaware corporations; if raising US institutional capital, a Delaware C-Corp may be preferred |
| **Activity licences** | Regulated sectors require additional licences from Estonian authorities [12] |
